# AI Policy

AI tools are welcome for code. We expect contributors to take responsibility for their work and speak in their own voice.

## Code

You may use AI to write, review, test, or debug code. Before submitting a contribution:

- Understand the changes and how they fit into the project.
- Review the code and test that it works.
- Be ready to explain your choices and address feedback yourself.

The same standards apply whether you write the code yourself or use AI assistance.

## Communication

Write issues, pull request descriptions, review comments, documentation, and discussions in your own words. We want to hear your experience, reasoning, and opinions. Don't post AI-generated replies or have an AI argue on your behalf.

You can use AI to research a topic, understand code, or explore ideas. The final text should be yours. Keep it concise, check your facts, and say when you are unsure.

Inspired by [Ghostty's AI policy](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md).
